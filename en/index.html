The Difference

Riveted into the extrusion.
Not into the wood.

Where others rivet into wood, the case wears out over time. We rivet directly into our robust, custom-developed extrusions and place our proprietary corners exactly where a case would otherwise yield first. The result: a case that lasts.

3 Years

Warranty on every case — made possible by our proven, proprietary extrusion and corner system.

One-Off

True custom manufacturing instead of indistinguishable off-the-shelf goods. Every case is tailored and immediately recognizable as a KALMS.

Sustainable

Our cases are built to last. Thanks to our full service, we repair them at any time — or rebuild them for your new equipment.

Rapid Prototyping & R&D

From concept to case in 5 days.

While large corporations are still flipping through catalogs, we are already building the prototype. For system integrators, research departments, and defense suppliers, we engineer cases with millimeter precision according to the strictest standards (e.g. MIL-STD-810H).

No rigid plastic standard dimensions. Uncompromising custom manufacturing, riveted directly into the extrusion – for extreme longevity in industry and the field.
