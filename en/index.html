FAQ

Before the first rivet.

A good case does not start in the workshop. It starts with clear coordination: dimensions, weight, connectors, handling, protection requirements, and operating environment.

How does KALMS make sure nothing gets misunderstood?

Before production, KALMS captures equipment dimensions, photos, drawings, connectors, handling points, center of gravity, weight, and operating environment in a structured way. Critical dimensions and fittings are clarified before manufacturing so the case, insert, and hardware fit the real equipment.

What information does KALMS need for a quotation?

Helpful inputs include outside dimensions, weight, sensitive areas, connectors, accessories, transport type, quantity, target date, and any existing drawings or photos. The better the input data, the more precisely KALMS can optimize size, stability, and weight.

How should I measure my equipment correctly?

Always measure the largest outside edges, including feet, handles, switches, sockets, and protruding parts. A photo with a ruler, a sketch, a datasheet, or a CAD file helps KALMS calculate padding, internal dimensions, and external dimensions correctly.

Standard case, partner solution, or custom build?

If a standard or partner case solves the requirement cleanly, KALMS recommends that route. If dimensions, internal fittings, weight, protection requirements, or handling do not fit, a custom case is engineered.

Can KALMS fit existing standard cases with custom inserts?

Yes. If the outer shell is already suitable, KALMS can design a new custom insert or adapt an existing standard case for different equipment. The robust shell stays in use while the sensitive interior is matched precisely to the device.

Can KALMS optimize weight, size, and stability?

Yes. Before manufacturing, KALMS evaluates the balance of stability, size, and weight. Materials, extrusions, corners, fittings, foam, and internal layout are selected to protect the equipment without making the case unnecessarily large or heavy.

Why does KALMS rivet into the extrusion instead of only into wood?

Wood can loosen or tear out under concentrated load. KALMS anchors critical connections directly in the stable extrusion and places proprietary corners where forces appear first. This distributes load more effectively and keeps the case dimensionally stable for longer.

Does KALMS build cases for unusual equipment?

Yes. KALMS specializes in one-off builds, prototypes, and special solutions. Unusual equipment, sensitive connectors, asymmetric shapes, and specific fitting positions can be taken into account.

Are 19-inch racks, vibration isolation, and in-case operation possible?

Yes, depending on the project. KALMS can plan rack depth, lid depth, panels, drawers, vibration isolators, ventilation, cable outlets, and service flaps when equipment needs to be transported or operated inside the case.

Are KALMS flightcases waterproof?

Traditional wood, extrusion, and fitting-based flightcases are built for hard transport and splash exposure, but they are not submersible IP67 boxes. If true sealing is required, KALMS can evaluate a suitable partner shell with a KALMS custom insert.

Is on-site measurement or consultation possible?

Yes. If photos, drawings, or datasheets are not enough, KALMS can clarify critical points directly and coordinate on-site measurement or consultation.

Can KALMS modify or repair existing cases?

Yes. Existing cases can be modified, repaired, or fitted with a new insert for different equipment if the base case is technically suitable.

How fast can KALMS deliver?

Delivery depends on scope, material, and coordination. With clear specifications, prototypes and small series become much easier to plan; the exact delivery date is confirmed after the requirements have been reviewed.

What helps with tenders and procurement?

A requirements document, deadlines, quantity, must-have criteria, requested proof, transport route, and existing technical files help a lot. KALMS can review early whether a custom build, standard solution, or no-bid decision is the right path before effort is spent on the wrong offer.
